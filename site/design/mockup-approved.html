Plant an app in three steps

One installer for every app on the farm. Paste your Tiiny key once; every app shares it and they take turns on the device.

1. Get the farmhandOne small tool, standard Python, nothing else.pip install tiinyapp-farm
2. Tell it about your TiinyBase URL and key from TiinyOS, Settings, API Key. Asked once, kept private on your machine.farm device
3. Plant and growInstall, start, stop, update. Your data stays in your plot.farm install titanium-tiiny-bot
farm start titanium-tiiny-bot

Bring your seeds

Built something for the Pocket Lab? Add one file to the farm and the farmhands check it before it goes in the field.

  • One manifest: name, what it does, what it needs, what it asks for, where the release lives.
  • The checks run on every seed: checksum, selfcheck, no secrets, permissions match the code.
  • Verified is earned by a person reading it, not by a robot.
The seedmanifests/your-app.jsonOpen a pull request on Titanium-Devops/tiinyapp-farm. That is the whole submission.
